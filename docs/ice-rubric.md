# ICE rubric

Score every idea from 1 to 10 on all three before it takes up production time. The same rubric sits in the workbook's **ICE Rubric** tab.

| Score | Impact: if it works, how far does it move the business? | Confidence: how strong is the evidence? | Ease: how cheaply can you make it well? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 to 10 | Likely to bring orders, leads or enquiries directly. | Proven on your own page at least twice. | Under an hour with templates you already have. |
| 7 to 8 | Builds trust that shows up in sales within weeks. | Worked once for you, or clearly works for a close competitor. | Half a day of work and no extra cost. |
| 5 to 6 | Grows reach or engagement, with an indirect link to sales. | A reasonable bet with some evidence. | About a day of work, or a small cost. |
| 3 to 4 | Nice to have. | Mostly a hunch. | Several days, or it needs outside help. |
| 1 to 2 | Unlikely to change anything you measure. | No evidence, only excitement. | Needs budget, people or skills you do not have yet. |

## How the score is calculated

**ICE score = ∛(Impact × Confidence × Ease)**, rounded to one decimal place.

This is the geometric mean of the three scores. It stays on the 1 to 10 scale, but one weak score pulls the whole idea down, which a simple average hides.

| Impact | Confidence | Ease | Simple average | ICE score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 9 | 2 | 6.7 | 5.5 |

## Priority tiers

| Tier | ICE score | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A · Build first | 7.0 or higher | Make these first. |
| B · Build | From 5.5 up to 7.0 | Make these when A ideas are covered. |
| C · Park | Below 5.5 | Revisit when the evidence or the budget changes. |

Change the tier thresholds on the **Read Me** tab.
